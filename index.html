Our Story

Small studio. Considered apps.

DataCraft Studio is an independent iOS studio. We’d rather build a few things well than a lot of things adequately — right now that’s fitr+ and travelr+, with more on the way. Every app we ship shares one account, keeps your data isolated app-by-app, and gets built by someone who actually wants it to exist.

fitr+

One app for training, food, and progress.

fitr+ tracks workouts, nutrition, biometrics, and goals — with an AI coach that actually knows your history, not just your last question.

Launching soon.

travelr+

Describe a trip. Get the whole itinerary.

travelr+ turns a rough idea into a full day-by-day plan — build it, browse it on a map, and refine it with AI. Same DataCraft Studio account as fitr+, none of your fitr+ data.

  • Day-by-day itineraries
  • Map view for every trip
  • AI-assisted refinement

In development.

One Account

Every app we build shares one account and nothing else. Your fitr+ data never touches travelr+, and vice versa — signing in once is just… signed in.
