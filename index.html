My Story

Small studio. Thoughtful apps.

I started DataCraft Studio to build the apps I kept wishing existed—thoughtful, beautifully designed tools that make complicated parts of life feel simpler.

Right now, I’m focused on fitr+ and travelr+: one brings your entire fitness journey together, while the other turns the idea of a trip into a plan you’re genuinely excited to take.

I care about the details: what deserves to be included, what should stay out, and where AI can be genuinely helpful instead of simply being added for the sake of it. Every feature has a reason to exist, and every app is something I would want to use myself.

This is a small studio by choice. Fewer apps, more attention, and no interest in building something forgettable.

fitr+

One app for training, food, and progress.

fitr+ tracks workouts, nutrition, biometrics, and goals — with an AI coach that actually knows your history, not just your last question.

Launching soon.

travelr+

Describe a trip. Get the whole itinerary.

travelr+ turns a rough idea into a full day-by-day plan — build it, browse it on a map, and refine it with AI. Same DataCraft Studio account as fitr+, none of your fitr+ data.

  • Day-by-day itineraries
  • Map view for every trip
  • AI-assisted refinement

In development.

One Account

Every app I build shares one account and nothing else. Your fitr+ data never touches travelr+, and vice versa — signing in once is just… signed in.
